## Reddit — r/admincraft

**Titulo:**
I made a server-side social mod for Fabric: bulletin boards, offline player mail and guilds — your players install nothing

**Cuerpo:**
Townsquare turns any lectern into a community board where players pin written books, adds /mail that works with offline recipients (attachments included), and simple guilds with /g chat.

The whole point is **zero client-side**: everything is vanilla lecterns, vanilla books and vanilla menus, so vanilla clients can join and use every feature. No database, no external services — it all lives in your world save.

Tested on a 1.21.11 dedicated server. Free, MIT, Fabric API is the only dependency.
